abstract: "Traditional forest fire detection models focus purely on drawing bounding boxes around smoke or flames. This approach suffers from two major bottlenecks: heavy models cannot run on cheap drones, and fast edge models trigger too many false alarms. This project proposes a Cognitive Fire Defense Pipeline—an asymmetric Edge-Cloud hybrid architecture tailored for real-world compute limits. We conduct two experiments across two data distributions to prove that a lightweight edge model acting as the Sentinel can be effectively paired with a zero-shot semantic pipeline acting as the Commander to achieve high accuracy without requiring heavy enterprise GPUs."
